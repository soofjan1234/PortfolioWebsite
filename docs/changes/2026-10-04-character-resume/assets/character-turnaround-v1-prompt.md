## 原始提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: character turnaround reference sheet for creating an original GLB bust model for a personal 3D resume website. Input image 1 is the identity and costume reference, not a background or texture to paste. Create one clean wide character modeling sheet with FOUR equally sized views of exactly the same person arranged from left to right: straight-on front view, three-quarter view, true side profile, back view. Show the entire head, neck, both shoulders and upper torso to mid-chest, keeping identical scale, height, pose and anatomy in all four views. Preserve the reference character's recognizable face, gently smiling closed mouth, large brown eyes, black rectangular full-rim glasses, tousled layered chestnut brown hair with side-swept fringe, warm light skin and light ivory crewneck sweatshirt. Translate the input into a polished sculpted 3D cartoon adult with smooth shaded volumes and physically plausible materials, suitable for cinematic closeups. Hair should consist of carefully sculpted curved clumps with restrained strand details, consistently reconstructable between views. Keep eyes as visibly rounded separate eyeballs with iris and pupil, glasses as clearly separate geometry, natural adult neck and shoulders. No stickers yet, no props, no NAS devices, no UI, no text, no labels, no logos, no watermark. Use a uniform neutral very light gray studio background, even soft neutral lighting, minimal shadows, no depth of field, no bloom, no extreme perspective. This is a modeling design reference, prioritize identity consistency, clear geometry and clean silhouette over mood.
